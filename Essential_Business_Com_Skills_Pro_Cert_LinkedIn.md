# Foundations of a Great Communicator
## Communicating with Confidence
### Confident communication is a skill
The way you speak is just a series of behaviors.
### The three elements of powerful communication
- Vocal 38%
- Visual 55%
- Verbal 7%
What adjectives do you want people to use to describe you?
- Energetic, authentic, fun, humorous, capable, easygoing, legend, friendly, intelligent
- "Tenho": Confiável, profissional, analítico, mente aberta, autêntico, ponderado, 
- "Desenvolver": Confiante, Acessível, Persuasivo, Envolvente
### Communication blocks that hold you back
- The spotlight effect
- Unrealistic expectations
- Lack of preparation
- Bad experience
- Brain vs. mouth connection
### The power of presence when you communicate
- Ask three follow-up questions
  - emotional intelligence
  - deepen relationships
- Listen with your eyes
  - body never lies
- Remove any distractions
## Manage Your Message
### Consider your audience when preparing to communicate
- So what? - rule or WIIFM (What is in for me?)
### Use the golden structure to organize your thought
- What do you want the audience to think, to feel, to do?
- What do you hope to achieve with the presentarion?
  - Purpose: Choose top three module from 6    
  - Introduction: Hook, 
      - Objective, 
      - Introduce yourserlf, 
      - Agenda
      - Modules, Ingridients (Studies, Statistic, Stories, Analogies, Metaphors, Similes, Pictures/Videos)
  - Conclusion: Stress the benefits, Summarize the modules, Call to Action (CTA)
## Develop Vocal Mastery
### If you breathe better, you sound better when speaking
### Vary your rate of speech to add clarity
- Slow down to highlight importance
### Use volume to radiate more confidence when speaking
- Proper volume emphasizes your presence, authority, vitality, enthusiasm, believe in what you are saying
### Make your message memorable with melody
## Develop Body Language Mastery
### Use eye contact effectively while communicating
- Don´t
  - Look over people's heads
  - Scan
  - Look sequentially
  - Look too long or stare
  - Dart your eyes
- Do
  - Look into people's eyes
  - Finish the thought with one person before moving to the next
  - Give good coverage
### Talk with your hands to show confidence
-  Satir
  - Placeter
  - Leveller
  - Blamer
  - Computer
  - Distractor
### Move with purpose and impact while communicating
- Vocal variety
- Physical movement - only move with purpose
  - Chanfe in time (Past on your right, future on your left)
  - Change in growth (right to the left)
  - Show sequence
